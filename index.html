PhraseKeep is a pocketbook for real-world language learning. Save phrases from menus, signs, conversations and announcements, then practice them with spaced repetition and native pronunciation until they stick.

Common questions

Saving phrases

What are the ways to save a phrase?

Tap + in the top corner and choose:

  • Type: with a bar of accented letters for your language, so you don't need another keyboard.
  • Camera or Photo: PhraseKeep reads the text in the picture. Tap the lines you want to keep.
  • Listen: records what you hear and writes it down as you go. The recording is kept so you can replay how a local said it.
  • Audio: pick a voice memo or other audio file to transcribe.

Each phrase can have a translation, a note, a category (like Dining or Transit) and the place you found it.

How do I save text from another app?

Select the text in Safari, Messages, Notes or almost any app, tap Share, then PhraseKeep. Add a translation if you like and tap Save. The phrase appears in your library the next time you open PhraseKeep.

Don't see PhraseKeep in the share sheet? Scroll the row of apps to the end, tap More, and turn it on.

How do I add the PhraseKeep widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and choose PhraseKeep. There are small, medium and large sizes. To customize one, touch and hold it and tap Edit Widget: choose your main capture method (Type, Camera, Photo, Listen or Audio file) and whether to show phrases due and your recent phrases.

Can PhraseKeep translate phrases for me?

Yes. Set Settings → Learning & audio → Translate into to your own language, and new phrases are translated automatically using Apple's on-device translation. A language may download once the first time. Anything you type in the translation yourself is never overwritten.

Why does PhraseKeep ask for my location?

So it can remember where you found each phrase, like "Boulangerie, Lyon". It's optional: turn it off in Settings → Capture, or decline the permission. Everything else still works.

Which languages are supported?

Arabic, Catalan, Chinese (Cantonese, Mandarin Simplified and Traditional), Croatian, Czech, Danish, Dutch (Netherlands and Belgium), English (US, UK, Australia, Canada, India, Ireland, New Zealand and South Africa), Finnish, French (France, Belgium, Canada and Switzerland), German (Germany, Austria and Switzerland), Greek, Hebrew, Hindi, Hungarian, Indonesian, Italian (Italy and Switzerland), Japanese, Korean, Malay, Norwegian, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), Romanian, Russian, Slovak, Spanish (Spain, Mexico, Latin America and the United States), Swedish, Thai, Turkish, Ukrainian and Vietnamese.

Choose the language you're learning in Settings → Learning & audio. Picking a regional accent means recordings are transcribed for that accent. You can also change the language of any single phrase.

Practicing

How does review work?

PhraseKeep uses spaced repetition: phrases come back just before you'd forget them. After each card, tap Again, Hard, Good or Easy. Each button shows when you'll see the phrase next. Phrases you miss come back later in the same session.

Can I change the voice or accent?

Yes. Go to Settings → Learning & audio → Voice & accent to choose any voice installed for your language, including other regions (for example a Canadian voice for French). PhraseKeep uses your iPhone's built-in voices, so to get more natural ones or more accents, download Enhanced or Premium voices in iOS Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices. They appear in PhraseKeep once downloaded. You can also change the speed, and turn on Auto-play pronunciation in Review.

Can PhraseKeep remind me to review?

Yes. Turn on Settings → Notifications → Daily review reminder and pick a time. You'll only get a reminder on days when phrases are due, with how many are waiting. Tap it to go straight to Review.

Transcription isn't working for my recording.

Make sure PhraseKeep can use speech recognition in iOS Settings → PhraseKeep. Some languages are transcribed on your iPhone and others need an internet connection. Your recording is saved either way, so you can type the phrase yourself.

PhraseKeep Pro

What does Pro include?

Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It adds:

  • the pronunciation coach, which compares your intonation with a native speaker's
  • Anki deck export with audio and photos
  • the Listen and Recall review modes
  • unlimited recordings (the free version keeps up to 25)

Capturing phrases, your library, Read reviews, the native voice, and CSV and Quizlet export are free.

I bought Pro but it isn't unlocked.

Go to Settings → PhraseKeep Pro → Restore purchases while signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy it. Pro is also shared with your Family Sharing group.

How do I use an offer code?

Go to Settings → PhraseKeep Pro → Redeem offer code, or tap Redeem code on the Pro screen. You can also redeem it in the App Store app from your account page.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Is my information private?

Yes. Your phrases, recordings, photos and places stay on your iPhone and, if iCloud Sync is on, in your own private iCloud, which we can't access. There are no accounts, ads or tracking. The only time anything reaches us is when you contact support. See the Privacy Policy.

Do my phrases sync between my iPhone and other devices?

Yes, with Settings → Data → iCloud Sync, which is on by default. Phrases, recordings and photos sync to every device signed in to the same Apple Account. The status under the switch shows when it last synced, or what's stopping it (for example, not being signed in to iCloud). If you turn it off, your phrases stay on this iPhone.

How do I export my phrases?

Go to Settings → Data → Export to Anki, CSV & Quizlet. You can export everything or one language at a time.

PhraseKeep says "Couldn't open your library."

Your phrases haven't been deleted, and PhraseKeep has saved a backup copy on your iPhone. Tap Contact support on that screen and include the backup if you can, so we can help you get your phrases back. Please don't choose "Start a fresh library" until you've heard from us.

How do I delete everything?

Go to Settings → Data → Delete all data. This permanently erases your phrases, recordings, photos and settings, and, if iCloud Sync is on, removes them from iCloud and your other devices too. Pro stays with your Apple Account and can be restored.

Accessibility

Does PhraseKeep work with VoiceOver and larger text?

Yes. PhraseKeep supports VoiceOver, Voice Control, Larger Text, Bold Text, Increase Contrast, Differentiate Without Color, Smart Invert and Reduce Motion, and lays out Arabic and Hebrew right to left. You can choose Light, Dark or Automatic appearance and turn off vibrations in Settings → Display & feedback. If something is hard to use, please tell us.
