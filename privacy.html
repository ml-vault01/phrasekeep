Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

The short version: your phrases, recordings, photos and places stay on your iPhone. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The only time anything reaches us is when you choose to contact support.

What stays on your device

Everything you save is stored only on your iPhone:

  • the phrases, translations and notes you type or capture
  • photos you take or choose, used to read text from signs, menus and tickets
  • audio clips you record or import
  • where you captured a phrase (a place name and map coordinates), if location tagging is on
  • your review progress, starred phrases and settings
  • text you send to PhraseKeep from other apps with the Share button

We run no servers for the app and never receive any of this. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or your computer, this data is included in that backup, as with any app.

Apple services the app uses

Some features rely on Apple system services, which follow Apple's Privacy Policy:

  • Text recognition in photos runs entirely on your device.
  • Speech recognition runs on your device for languages that support it. For other languages, iOS may send the audio to Apple to transcribe it. Apple doesn't share it with us.
  • Translation suggestions use Apple's Translation service.
  • Place names: when location tagging is on, your device sends the location to Apple's map service to look up a place name (for example "Boulangerie, Lyon"). You can turn this off in Settings → Capture, or deny location access.
  • Purchases and tips are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Contacting support

If you send a message from Settings → Help → Contact support (or from the "Couldn't open your library" screen), we receive the name, email address, subject and message you enter, plus your app version, iOS version and device type. The message is delivered to our inbox through Web3Forms, a form-to-email service. If you send it with Mail instead, it goes through your own email provider. If you choose to attach a library backup, we also receive the phrases in it.

We use this only to reply to you and fix the problem. We don't add you to mailing lists, and we delete support emails when they're no longer needed. Your name and email are also remembered on your device so you don't have to retype them next time; Delete all data clears them.

Permissions

PhraseKeep asks for the camera, microphone, speech recognition and location only when you first use the feature that needs it. You can change these at any time in iOS Settings → PhraseKeep.

Sharing and exports

PhraseKeep shares nothing on its own. When you export a deck or CSV, or copy phrases for Quizlet, the data goes only where you choose to send it.

Deleting your data

Settings → Your data → Delete all data permanently erases every phrase, recording, photo and setting on your device. Deleting the app does the same. Purchases stay with your Apple Account and can be restored. To have a support conversation deleted, email us and we'll remove it.

Children

PhraseKeep isn't directed at children under 13. The only personal information we ever receive is what someone chooses to send us through support. If you believe a child has sent us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. If a change means more data would leave your device, we'll explain it in the app before it happens.
